Meet the Services

Understand the microservices that make up Runtime Roasters.

The Actors

Runtime Roasters is a microservice application. Rather than a single giant server, multiple specialized services cooperate to fulfill requests.

Knowing which service owns what data makes it easy to instruct AI where to add features. For example, driver GPS tracking belongs to the logistics service, not the retail service.

Roles & Responsibilities

🛡️
Auth Service

Checks identities and enforces rules on who can read/write what.

☕
Retail Service

Manages menus, stores, transactions, and cup purchases.

🚚
Logistics Service

Tracks warehouse shipments, delivery routes, and driver GPS coordinates.

The Theater Metaphor

Think of microservices like a theater production. Instead of one actor trying to play all roles, you have specialized actors (Retail, Auth, Logistics) reading from the same script. If one actor catches a cold (crashes), the others can still perform their scenes, making the system highly robust.
